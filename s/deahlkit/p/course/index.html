The course

Every module has two editions: one for managers, one for healthcare professionals. Open a module to read it slide by slide, or play it the way a learner meets it.

Play the course ↗ The films on YouTube ↗

Six modules

  • 1 · Introduction
  • 2 · Leadership and organisational commitment
  • 3 · Clear communication, information and guidance
  • 4 · Addressing vulnerable groups
  • 5 · Accessibility, findability and usability
  • 6 · Fostering trust in digital health services

72 modules, 448 films

An English master and six language versions, each with twelve modules. The 64 films of each language are published on YouTube with subtitles and linked from the course.

DEAHLKit is a digital health literacy e-learning course produced by the DEAHL Baltic consortium, co-funded by Interreg Baltic Sea Region and the European Union.

Run and managed by the Innovation and Development Unit, Ängelholms hospital, and Innovation Skåne · Region Skåne
