Leaders · age 44

Digital Health Implementation Lead, regional health authority

Led the regional roll-out on time and on budget. Chose to keep front-line staff out, believing it would slow things down. Eight weeks after go-live he is starting to see that delivery and implementation are not the same.

“I've delivered this platform to spec. But I'm starting to think 'to spec' wasn't the right target.”
  • Goes from confident project lead to reflective practitioner
  • Knows how a better roll-out would look
  • Sits between strategy and the front line

Usually found in: Karen's office.
