## Context
I am writing a short film script for [PROJECT], for [AUDIENCE]. The aim is that learners [AIM].

## Shared case universe
The script uses the Northviken case universe (version 2.1, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- Setting: Northviken Health and Care Centre, a public integrated health facility. Scene: Home, evening.
- Platform: DEAHL-Connect: video consultations and patient questionnaires. It is competent but under-supported.
- Time: eight weeks after go-live. The figures look fine; the front line is more complicated.
- Scene to build on: P, Miriam's question about fetal movements. A clinical scale with no explanation, forty minutes of searching, a low score. Fatima calls the next morning; Miriam is already at the doctor's.

## The people
- Miriam, 33, Works part-time in retail, pregnant, carer for her father. Twenty-six weeks pregnant with her first child, and asked to use the platform for her antenatal questionnaires. She also helps her father, 75, who attends rheumatology, but cannot reach his account. Speaks three languages. Typically says: "When a questionnaire asks me something I don't understand, I don't know if not knowing means something is wrong."
- Fatima, 38, Senior Midwife and Midwifery Team Lead. Fourteen years a midwife, six at Northviken. Follows women from booking to six weeks after birth, at the clinic, at home and on the maternity unit, and ran her own video check-ins before the platform came. The platform was set up for specialist clinics, not for her. Typically says: "I've been doing video midwifery for three years with a free app and a prayer. Now I have less functionality than before."

## Requirements
- Length: about [3 to 4] minutes.
- Tone: realistic, not dramatised. A partial resolution, not a tidy fix.
- End with an open question for the learner.

## Do not
- Invent new people, or change what is established about these.
- Name a real country, city or platform.
- Make the technology the problem.

## Output
A screenplay: scene directions in brackets, the speaker's name before each line, at most three people on screen at a time, lines of two to three sentences. Then a short scene-by-scene summary for the production team.
