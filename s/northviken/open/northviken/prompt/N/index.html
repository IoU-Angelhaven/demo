## Context
I am writing a short film script for [PROJECT], for [AUDIENCE]. The aim is that learners [AIM].

## Shared case universe
The script uses the Northviken case universe (version 2.1, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- Setting: Northviken Health and Care Centre, a public integrated health facility. Scene: Rehabilitation gym.
- Platform: DEAHL-Connect: video consultations and patient questionnaires. It is competent but under-supported.
- Time: eight weeks after go-live. The figures look fine; the front line is more complicated.
- Scene to build on: N, Elena's discovery. The platform has the measures her team keeps on paper. She finds the manual, but has no access.

## The people
- Elena, 37, Physiotherapist and Rehabilitation Team Lead. Leads physiotherapists, an occupational therapist and a part-time psychologist who work on the ward, in clinics, by video and in the community. Her team has measured pain and function on paper for years. Nobody told them the platform did the same. Typically says: "DEAHL-Connect does exactly what we already do. No one told us it existed until three weeks after it went live."

## Requirements
- Length: about [3 to 4] minutes.
- Tone: realistic, not dramatised. A partial resolution, not a tidy fix.
- End with an open question for the learner.

## Do not
- Invent new people, or change what is established about these.
- Name a real country, city or platform.
- Make the technology the problem.

## Output
A screenplay: scene directions in brackets, the speaker's name before each line, at most three people on screen at a time, lines of two to three sentences. Then a short scene-by-scene summary for the production team.
