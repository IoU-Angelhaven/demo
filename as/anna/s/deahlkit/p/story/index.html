The story

Placeholder for the story of how DEAHLKit was made: partners in seven countries, national teams that reviewed text, speech and films in their own language, and a production that had to be coordinated across all of them at once.

The use of AI in the production, including synthetic speech in each language, is accounted for openly.

The story is told as the project goes on: the pilot in autumn 2026, what the pilot showed, and the final version.

Demonstration page in the prototype. The official Interreg Baltic Sea Region logo and funding statement are to be added according to the programme's rules.
