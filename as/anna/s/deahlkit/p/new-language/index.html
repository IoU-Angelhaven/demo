Your language

A new language version starts with a conversation. DEAHLKit is built to travel. Tell us about your country, your language and your learners. Then we choose together the way that fits you. There are two ways, and the same agreement for both.

Two ways

  • Way 1 · Do it yourselves. Your team makes the version with the same tools, master and guides the first seven languages were made with. We give guidance, access to the Studio and the up-to-date English master.
  • Way 2 · Get help from the DEAHL team. The DEAHL production team builds the version with you: translation support, audio, edited and published films and course packages.

The steps

  • Translation · Translation proposals for every text, with agreed terms for the whole course.
  • Review · Your national team reviews every text and spoken line in the Studio.
  • Audio · Every character's lines are recorded in your language.
  • Films · The 64 films are rebuilt from the English master films, with the new audio and on-screen text.
  • Packages · The course in your language, online or ready for your own platform.

Whichever way you choose, you agree that your language version

  • stays in line with the English master and follows its updates;
  • is published and made available for use in the same way as the other language versions (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with DEAHL as publisher);
  • has a maintenance plan for future updates when the master changes;
  • has two named national contact persons, connected to the DEAHL production team.
