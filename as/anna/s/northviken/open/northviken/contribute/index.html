Pictures and voices: examples, not requirements

In DEAHLKit, the first project to use Northviken, the people were animated in Vyond and given voices with ElevenLabs. Those figures and voices are shown here as examples. You may use them if your own licences for those tools allow it, or go another way: any animation tool, your own drawings, photos, actors or other voices. What makes someone Northviken's Karen is how she is described, not which tool drew her.

Northviken was developed by IoU through iterative prompting and dialogue with an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic). People chose, edited and reviewed everything. Additions made with AI through the proposal flow say so in the same way.

You may

  • Use any person, place, scene or moment in the timeline, without asking
  • Add details that fit what is already established
  • Write new scenes with the people and places that exist
  • Make your own pictures, animations and voices of the people, with any tool
  • Adapt names to a language, such as Nordviken in Swedish
  • Add details for a country, as long as they add and do not replace

You may not

  • Contradict what is established about a person, a place or the timeline
  • Place Northviken in a real country, city or region
  • Let DEAHL-Connect stand for a real platform or vendor
  • Make the technology the problem: DEAHL-Connect is competent but under-supported
  • Resolve a scene another project has left open on purpose

Propose something new

Want a new person, place, scene or phase in the timeline? Propose it. The editorial team makes it fit, writes the description, and if you like adds an example figure and voice, then runs a feedback round before it joins the canon.

  5. In the canon
  • A personSomeone who works at Northviken, or a patient or carer.
  • A placeA clinic, a unit or a room, for example a cancer care clinic.
  • A scene or caseA moment with a place, a few people and a tension.
  • An event or a new phaseSomething that changes Northviken, for example a later period in the timeline.

Adapt it to a country

  • DenmarkDEAHL-Connect can be placed in the Min Sundhed and sundhed.dk landscape, the authority read as a Danish region, home visits as municipal home care.
  • FinlandDEAHL-Connect can be placed beside Kanta and OmaKanta, the authority read as a wellbeing services county. Lars can be Finnish-Swedish.
  • GermanyDEAHL-Connect can be placed in the Telematikinfrastruktur and ePA context. Real-world reference: Hamburg's digital strategy (as of 2026).
  • LatviaDEAHL-Connect can be placed in the E-veselība context, with family carers in a central role. Real-world reference: the digital transformation strategy of Strenči psychoneurological hospital (as of 2026).
  • PolandDEAHL-Connect can be placed beside the Internetowe Konto Pacjenta, the authority read as an NFZ district. Miriam's and Radu's languages and access matter here.
  • SwedenNorthviken can be called Nordviken. DEAHL-Connect can be placed beside 1177, the authority read as a Swedish region. Real-world reference: Ängelholm Hospital's digitalisation strategy 2026–2029.
