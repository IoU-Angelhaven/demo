Northviken grows with every project. This is the version history, newest first.

  1. Version 2.1 · A place: Example: a cancer care clinic · from Example project on cancer rehabilitation · 2026-11-25
  2. Version 2.0 · IoU · 2026-11-24
    Northviken becomes a shared universe on the platform, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Northviken is described as an integrated health facility able to hold any kind of care. Names may be adapted per language. Contradictions in the timeline and the figures are corrected, and the references to DEAHLKit's module numbers are removed.
  3. Version 1.2 · DEAHL Baltic · 2026
    The case manuscript used for DEAHLKit: 17 people, 17 scenes, four care settings and DEAHL-Connect. Fatima and Miriam's pregnancy were added during the work.

Northviken case universe, created by the Innovation and Development Unit (IoU), Ängelholm Hospital, Region Skåne, within the Interreg Baltic Sea Region project DEAHL Baltic. Developed through iterative prompting and dialogue with AI (Claude, Anthropic).

Licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Northviken, its people and DEAHL-Connect are fictional. The example figures and voices follow the terms of the tools they were made with.
