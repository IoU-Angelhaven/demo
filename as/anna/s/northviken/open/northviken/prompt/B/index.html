## Context
I am writing a short film script for [PROJECT], for [AUDIENCE]. The aim is that learners [AIM].

## Shared case universe
The script uses the Northviken case universe (version 2.1, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- Setting: Northviken Health and Care Centre, a public integrated health facility. Scene: Rheumatology consulting room.
- Platform: DEAHL-Connect: video consultations and patient questionnaires. It is competent but under-supported.
- Time: eight weeks after go-live. The figures look fine; the front line is more complicated.
- Scene to build on: B, Dr. Amara and the fatigue scale. Ingrid arrives with the form half done. Dr. Amara tries to explain the fatigue scale, then spends eleven minutes entering data twice.

## The people
- Dr. Amara, 34, Specialist Registrar, Rheumatology. Trained in another country and joined two years ago. Clinically strong and quietly one of the most digitally able on the team. What she was never given is the words and the guidance to use the platform with patients. Typically says: "I didn't know there was guidance built into the platform. If I'd known, I could have shown her."
- Ingrid, 64, Retired teacher, rheumatology patient. Has rheumatoid arthritis and comes for review every four months. Uses her phone for video calls, banking and news. Her worry about the questionnaire is not about technology. It is about trust. Typically says: "I just needed someone to tell me that my answers would only be seen by my doctor and that there were no wrong answers."

## Requirements
- Length: about [3 to 4] minutes.
- Tone: realistic, not dramatised. A partial resolution, not a tidy fix.
- End with an open question for the learner.

## Do not
- Invent new people, or change what is established about these.
- Name a real country, city or platform.
- Make the technology the problem.

## Output
A screenplay: scene directions in brackets, the speaker's name before each line, at most three people on screen at a time, lines of two to three sentences. Then a short scene-by-scene summary for the production team.
