DEAHLKit

Northviken was created for DEAHLKit, a course on digital health literacy for healthcare. Its learners meet Ingrid at her kitchen table, Dr. Amara without the words to explain, and Karen looking at figures that look fine. The same people return in every module. The course is the place to see the universe in depth.

Take the course ↗

Make your own Northviken e-learning

DEAHLKit's modules were built with an e-learning builder that is now being moved into the platform's shared layer. With it, a project can make its own Northviken e-learning: from PowerPoint to H5P or SCORM, with pictures that keep their source and licence, quizzes, narration, film and subtitles.
