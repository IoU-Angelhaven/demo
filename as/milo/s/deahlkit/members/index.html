The DEAHLKit Studio

The Studio is where the language teams and the production team work together. It is in daily use on its own address, with its own sign-in. On the shared platform its accounts move to the common sign-in.

You are signed in as Milo Member. Your role here is Reviewer.

In this project your role can:

  • Read the notes in the workspace
  • Comment and suggest
  • Save new versions

What happens in this workspace

  • 1 · English masterAn editor proposes a change, an admin approves it and publishes a new master version. The change is marked for review in every language that has the text.
  • 2 · New language versionsA language takes the course in: translation, speech, films and course text. Reviewers correct the text in the browser, and only the lines that change are recorded again.
  • 3 · Published language versionsThe reference copy of each published language. An editing round is opened per language, and closed again.
  • Review per languageText, speech and films are reviewed and approved line by line, with comments.
  • Editing in the browserLanguage teams edit layout, text and menu titles. Every change is saved as a named version and can be undone.
  • Films and subtitlesFinal films per language in module order, with subtitles checked against the approved text.
  • AI transparencyOne place where the use of AI and synthetic speech is accounted for, per language.
  • E-learning builder (lab)From PowerPoint to H5P, with image bank, quizzes, read-aloud text, film and subtitles. To become a shared tool.

Tools

  • Shared notesWrite together. Every save is a named version that can be restored.
