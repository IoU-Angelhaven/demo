DEAHL-Connect is Northviken's fictional platform for patient contact. It combines video consultations with questionnaires that patients fill in before appointments (e-PROM and e-PREM). The regional health authority procured it eighteen months ago, and it went live at Northviken eight weeks ago.

DEAHL-Connect is not the villain. It is a competent platform that underperforms because the conditions for using it have not been built. The case is about the gap between technical go-live and human readiness.

What it does

  • Video consultations in the browser or the patient app, for follow-ups, medication reviews, rehabilitation and care coordination
  • Questionnaires before appointments or on a schedule, with the answers visible to clinicians before and during the consultation

What does not work yet

  • The link to the patient record is incomplete, so some data is entered twice
  • The questionnaires use clinical language that has not been simplified
  • Guidance for clinicians exists in the platform, but hardly anyone knows it is there
  • Patients get an SMS with a link and a login, and nothing else
  • Video works on modern phones and computers, not on older devices
  • Carers cannot act for a patient: proxy access is not switched on
  • Ward and emergency staff are not in the platform's user registry

Three groups, one gap

  • Patients and carersare asked to fill in a questionnaire about their health without knowing what the questions mean, who sees the answers or what follows from them.
  • Health professionalsare asked to introduce the platform, explain the questionnaires and read the scores without any training in that side of the work.
  • Managersare asked to report success from adoption figures that say nothing about the quality of care, equal access or the strain on staff.
