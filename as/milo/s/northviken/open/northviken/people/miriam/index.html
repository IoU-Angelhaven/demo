M

Patients and carers · age 33

Works part-time in retail, pregnant, carer for her father

Twenty-six weeks pregnant with her first child, and asked to use the platform for her antenatal questionnaires. She also helps her father, 75, who attends rheumatology, but cannot reach his account. Speaks three languages.

“When a questionnaire asks me something I don't understand, I don't know if not knowing means something is wrong.”
  • Uses the platform both as a patient and as a carer
  • Her anxiety comes from the questionnaire's design, not her skills
  • Shows the need for proxy access and other languages

Usually found in: Reception desk, Ingrid's kitchen, Home visits and the community.

Scenes

  • Scene F: Miriam cannot log inMiriam with her father, Sofia on the phone. No proxy access, no solution. The appointment goes ahead without the answers.
  • Scene P: Miriam's question about fetal movementsA clinical scale with no explanation, forty minutes of searching, a low score. Fatima calls the next morning; Miriam is already at the doctor's.
