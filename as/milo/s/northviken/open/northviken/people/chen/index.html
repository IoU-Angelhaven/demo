Clinical staff · age 39

Senior Clinician, Emergency Department

Works shifts in a department where tools must work at once or get out of the way. Patients leaving for follow-up are asked to activate DEAHL-Connect, and she cannot explain it. She sends them to reception.

“I tell them to ask at reception. What else am I supposed to do?”
  • Outside the roll-out, so without knowledge or mandate
  • Stands at the transition where digital onboarding fails most
  • Would need a one-page briefing and a discharge routine

Usually found in: Emergency department.
