The Northviken editorial room

Where proposals for Northviken are made to fit the canon, given a description, a figure and a voice, and discussed in feedback rounds before they join the canon. Contributors from every project that uses Northviken take part in the feedback rounds.

You are signed in as Milo Member. Your role here is Contributor.

  • Read the proposals in the workspace
  • Comment in the feedback rounds

Tools

  • NorthvikenA shared case universe: a fictional integrated health facility, its people and its digital platform, free to use in e-learning, film and exercises.

Northviken case universe, created by the Innovation and Development Unit (IoU), Ängelholm Hospital, Region Skåne, within the Interreg Baltic Sea Region project DEAHL Baltic. Developed through iterative prompting and dialogue with AI (Claude, Anthropic).
