What the project plans to build

Everything on this page is planned and depends on the application being approved.

A network with a shared workspace

  • An open layer with the AMBeR model, local guides, stories, e-learning and a way to submit needs or ideas.
  • A members' layer with an initiative board (idea, hypothesis, prototype, test, spread) and working materials.
  • Open to new partners and stakeholders, and meant to live on after the project period.

The AMBeR model in use

  • A trained local facilitator in every partner organisation.
  • An e-learning course on implementation in all partner languages, made with the method from the DEAHL Baltic project.

A self-assessment tool for digital readiness

  • Local pathways in cancer rehabilitation are mapped.
  • A tool lets an organisation assess how ready it is for digital cancer rehabilitation, made available across the South Baltic region.

AMBeR Academy and Practice Labs

  • Training, workshops and study visits in digital cancer rehabilitation for health care professionals across the region.

AMBeR BRIDGE is a proposed project. An application was submitted to the Interreg South Baltic Programme in November 2026, and no funding decision has been made.

Run and managed by the Innovation and Development Unit, Ängelholms hospital, and Innovation Skåne · Region Skåne
