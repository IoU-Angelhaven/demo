The workspace: AMBeR BRIDGE

AMBeR BRIDGE is where the partners keep working together after the project: initiatives on a board, documents with status and version history, discussions and "Ask the network", meetings with decisions and actions, the organisations in the network, local guides and courses.

Today it is a clickable prototype with example data. Its roles are administrator, member and associated partner. On the shared platform they are the standard roles, with AMBeR's own names.
