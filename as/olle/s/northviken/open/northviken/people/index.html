Pictures and voices: examples, not requirements

In DEAHLKit, the first project to use Northviken, the people were animated in Vyond and given voices with ElevenLabs. Those figures and voices are shown here as examples. You may use them if your own licences for those tools allow it, or go another way: any animation tool, your own drawings, photos, actors or other voices. What makes someone Northviken's Karen is how she is described, not which tool drew her.
