R

Patients and carers · age 38

Works part-time, mental health patient

Two years in outpatient mental health care after a depression that needed inpatient care. In recovery and rebuilding his routine. He did not fill in the questionnaire, feared his appointment would be cancelled, and did not say so.

“I didn't know if not completing the form would affect my appointment. I thought maybe they'd cancel it.”
  • Clear, predictable paths calm him; unclear ones do the opposite
  • His fear is a communication failure, not his
  • Raises privacy, consent and trust in digital care

Scenes

  • Scene E: Radu and the missed questionnaireRadu arrives without the form. His clinician does not ask why, and Radu does not tell. Left open on purpose.
