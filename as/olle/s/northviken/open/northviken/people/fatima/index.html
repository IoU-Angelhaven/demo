F

Clinical staff · age 38

Senior Midwife and Midwifery Team Lead

Fourteen years a midwife, six at Northviken. Follows women from booking to six weeks after birth, at the clinic, at home and on the maternity unit, and ran her own video check-ins before the platform came. The platform was set up for specialist clinics, not for her.

“I've been doing video midwifery for three years with a free app and a prayer. Now I have less functionality than before.”
  • Works in all four parts of Northviken
  • More practical digital experience than almost anyone
  • Shows what happens when a roll-out ignores existing practice

Usually found in: Home visits and the community.

Scenes

  • Scene P: Miriam's question about fetal movementsA clinical scale with no explanation, forty minutes of searching, a low score. Fatima calls the next morning; Miriam is already at the doctor's.
  • Scene Q: Fatima's missing functionsNo home-visit template, no antenatal questionnaire, not in the registry. She e-mails Karen: the start of a maternity working group.
