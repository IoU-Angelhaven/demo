S

Support and enabling staff · age 29

Receptionist and Patient Navigator

The person patients call when the platform confuses them, while she checks people in and answers everything else. The role has grown since go-live without being recognised. Speaks three languages.

“I've started keeping a list of the things patients ask me about the platform. Nobody reads it.”
  • First to know about confusion, last to be asked
  • Her list is the best improvement data in the building
  • The informal contact for patients who speak other languages

Usually found in: Small meeting room, Reception desk.
