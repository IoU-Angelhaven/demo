The course

Six modules, each in two editions: one for managers and one for healthcare professionals.

The modules: 1 Introduction · 2 Leadership and organisational commitment · 3 Clear communication, information and guidance · 4 Addressing vulnerable groups · 5 Accessibility, findability and usability · 6 Fostering trust in digital health services.

An English master and six language versions with finished pilot versions: Danish, Swedish, Finnish, German, Polish and Latvian. That is 72 e-learning modules in six languages.

448 films, with subtitles in every language, published on the project's YouTube channel.

DEAHLKit is a digital health literacy e-learning course produced by the DEAHL Baltic consortium, co-funded by Interreg Baltic Sea Region and the European Union.

Run and managed by the Innovation and Development Unit, Ängelholms hospital, and Innovation Skåne · Region Skåne
