DEAHLKit

E-learning on digital health literacy for managers and healthcare professionals: an English master and six language versions.

DEAHLKit is the e-learning kit on digital health literacy made by the DEAHL Baltic consortium in the Interreg Baltic Sea Region programme.

Here the project presents the course in all its languages, the story of how it was made, and the offer to others who want to use it or take it into their own language. The texts are a first draft for the demo.

The six language versions are finished pilot versions. The course is being pilot-tested in autumn 2026.

  • The courseSix modules, each in two editions: one for managers and one for healthcare professionals.
