AMBeR BRIDGE: Baltic Regional Initiative on Digitally Guided Exercise oncology

A cross-border learning network for digitally guided physical rehabilitation in cancer care, building on the AMBeR project.

AMBeR BRIDGE is a proposed project. The application was submitted to the Interreg South Baltic Programme in November 2026 and no decision has been made. If it is approved, the project runs from June 2027 to May 2029.

The aim is to turn the AMBeR partnership into a lasting cross-border learning network for digitally guided physical rehabilitation in cancer care, open to professionals, managers, patient organisations and other local actors, and to bring clinical practice closer to international standards.

The AMBeR project showed that patients are willing and able to use digital services in rehabilitation. What holds implementation back is knowledge, the capacity to change ways of working, and networks where organisations learn from each other. That is what AMBeR BRIDGE is for.
