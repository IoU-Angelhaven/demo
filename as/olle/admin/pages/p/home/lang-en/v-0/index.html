Top of the page

Heading

Putting e-health into practice, together

Description

ehealth-implementation.eu gathers results and tools from European projects that help health care organisations introduce digital ways of working. Everything on the open pages is free to read and use.

Each project has its own space here. Choose a project to see what it has produced, or go straight to its tools.

Project cards

Text on the card for AMBeR

Knowledge, tools and experiences from the AMBeR project to help healthcare organisations in the Baltic Sea region implement eHealth in healthcare.

Text on the card for DEAHLKit

A course for healthcare in the digital age: six modules on digital health literacy, each in two editions, in seven languages.

Text on the card for DigiH4A · Digital Health for All

Health authorities, academic institutions and innovators develop new ways for digital health tools to be reimbursed, and methods for assessing their costs and benefits.

Text on the card for AMBeR BRIDGE

A cross-border learning network for digitally guided physical rehabilitation in cancer care, building on the AMBeR project.

Text on the card for Northviken

A shared case universe: a fictional integrated health facility, its people and its digital platform, free to use in e-learning, film and exercises, and open for every project to build on.
