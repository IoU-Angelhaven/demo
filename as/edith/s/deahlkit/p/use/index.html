Use it and spread it

Use the course as it is: the modules can be exported to H5P and to SCORM for other learning platforms.

Take it into your own language: DEAHLKit comes with a tool and a model for how a new language makes its own version, with translation, speech, films and course text. It has been tried with Estonian, Lithuanian and Norwegian.

Stay in step: a change in the English master is made once and is marked for review in every language version.

The terms for using DEAHLKit are decided by the DEAHL Baltic steering group.

DEAHLKit is a digital health literacy e-learning course produced by the DEAHL Baltic consortium, co-funded by Interreg Baltic Sea Region and the European Union.

Run and managed by the Innovation and Development Unit, Ängelholms hospital, and Innovation Skåne · Region Skåne
