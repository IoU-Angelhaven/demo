Get it for your learners

DEAHLKit is made to be reused. Every language version can be brought into the systems your organisation already uses, or used as it is, online.

Three ways to bring DEAHLKit to your learners

  • Import · H5P files into your platform. One file per module and edition, imported into any learning platform that supports H5P, for example Moodle, WordPress or Canvas. You host it yourself.
  • Embed · SCORM in your learning system. The modules packaged for learning management systems that take SCORM, so completion can be followed in your own system.
  • Online · Open on DEAHL.eu. Every language version as open e-learning on the DEAHL website, with nothing to install.

Tell us where it will be used

To fetch the course files we ask who you are and where the course will be used. Then we get back to you with the files for your language and format.
