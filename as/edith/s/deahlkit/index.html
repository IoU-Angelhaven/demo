DEAHLKit: a course for healthcare in the digital age

A course for healthcare in the digital age: six modules on digital health literacy, each in two editions, in seven languages.

Six modules, each in two editions (for healthcare managers and for healthcare professionals) about communication, vulnerable groups, accessibility, trust and leadership when health services go digital. Short films from the fictional Northviken Health and Care Centre, reflection and quizzes, in seven languages.

For the partners: The DEAHLKit Studio

The Studio is where the language teams and the production team work together. It is in daily use on its own address, with its own sign-in. On the shared platform its accounts move to the common sign-in.

DEAHLKit is a digital health literacy e-learning course produced by the DEAHL Baltic consortium, co-funded by Interreg Baltic Sea Region and the European Union.

Run and managed by the Innovation and Development Unit, Ängelholms hospital, and Innovation Skåne · Region Skåne
