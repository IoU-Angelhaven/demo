Most stories start eight weeks after go-live. The timeline shows how Northviken got there, and where it is heading.

  1. 18 months beforeProcurementThe regional health authority chooses DEAHL-Connect. Oliver leads the project. Front-line staff at Northviken are not involved.
  2. 6 weeks beforeTrainingThe vendor gives every clinician two hours on how to use the system, and nothing on how to talk to patients about it. Karen signs off the training report.
  3. Go-liveDEAHL-Connect goes liveThe figures start to come in, and so do the calls to reception. Sofia starts her list. Jonas files his ticket about the record link.
  4. 1 week afterIngrid and RaduIngrid gets the SMS before her follow-up and gives up halfway. Radu does not fill in his questionnaire and does not say why.
  5. 2 weeks afterIngrid's appointment, Miriam's two attemptsDr. Amara explains one scale; that evening Karen reports 68 and 91 per cent. Miriam cannot reach her father's account, and is frightened by a question in her own antenatal questionnaire.
  6. 3 weeks afterElena and FatimaElena finds that the platform does what her team does on paper, but her team is not in the registry. Fatima finds no home-visit template and no antenatal questionnaire, and e-mails Karen.
  7. 4 weeks afterAnna reads the SMSA colleague forwards a social media post about the clinic's message. Anna starts rewriting it, without being asked.
  8. 6 weeks afterKaren convenes the working groupKaren, Dr. Amara, Lars, Sofia and Ingrid on screen. Three findings in an hour, and the start of an action plan.
  9. 8 weeks afterNowThings work, but not as well as they should. Some improvements are under way. There is no structure for peer learning yet, and Jonas's ticket is unanswered.
  10. 6 months afterReviewThe hoped-for future: better, clearly still ongoing. Oliver writes 'What we would do differently'.
