## Context
I am writing a short film script for [PROJECT], for [AUDIENCE]. The aim is that learners [AIM].

## Shared case universe
The script uses the Northviken case universe (version 2.1, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- Setting: Northviken Health and Care Centre, a public integrated health facility. Scene: Jonas's desk.
- Platform: DEAHL-Connect: video consultations and patient questionnaires. It is competent but under-supported.
- Time: eight weeks after go-live. The figures look fine; the front line is more complicated.
- Scene to build on: K, Jonas's eight-week ticket. 'Under review' for eight weeks. He writes an e-mail to Karen, and deletes it.

## The people
- Jonas, 35, IT and Medical Technology Specialist. Runs the local IT and is the link to the regional unit that runs the platform. He knows exactly what is broken. He filed a ticket about the record link eight weeks ago, and is still waiting. Typically says: "I know what's broken. The question is who has the authority to fix it, and that person is three governance layers above me."
- Karen, 47, Outpatient Services Manager. Nine years at Northviken, four in her current role. Came up through nursing, respected by her staff and trusted by senior management. The person who makes things happen, and who carries the weight when they stall. Typically says: "These aren't KPI problems. These are readiness problems."

## Requirements
- Length: about [3 to 4] minutes.
- Tone: realistic, not dramatised. A partial resolution, not a tidy fix.
- End with an open question for the learner.

## Do not
- Invent new people, or change what is established about these.
- Name a real country, city or platform.
- Make the technology the problem.

## Output
A screenplay: scene directions in brackets, the speaker's name before each line, at most three people on screen at a time, lines of two to three sentences. Then a short scene-by-scene summary for the production team.
