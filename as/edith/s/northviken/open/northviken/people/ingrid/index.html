I

Patients and carers · age 64

Retired teacher, rheumatology patient

Has rheumatoid arthritis and comes for review every four months. Uses her phone for video calls, banking and news. Her worry about the questionnaire is not about technology. It is about trust.

“I just needed someone to tell me that my answers would only be seen by my doctor and that there were no wrong answers.”
  • Confident with technology, new to health platforms
  • Afraid her answers will be used against her
  • Would take part in improving the service if asked

Usually found in: Rheumatology consulting room, Small meeting room, Reception desk, Ingrid's kitchen.
