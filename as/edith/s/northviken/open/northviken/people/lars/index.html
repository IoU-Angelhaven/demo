L

Clinical staff · age 52

Senior Nurse, Rheumatology outpatient clinic

Nineteen years at Northviken and the clinic's memory. Has seen platforms come and go. Cautious, not resistant, and ready to engage if someone asks him seriously.

“The patients who come in already anxious are the ones who don't complete the form. Could we build in five minutes at the start?”
  • Knows which patients struggle, and nobody has asked
  • A natural peer mentor without a structure for it
  • Names the practical fix management missed

Usually found in: Rheumatology consulting room, Small meeting room, Staff room and corridor.
