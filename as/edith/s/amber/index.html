AMBeR: Advanced Modeling of Baltic Cancer E-Care

Knowledge, tools and experiences from the AMBeR project to help healthcare organisations in the Baltic Sea region implement eHealth in healthcare.

AMBeR is an Interreg South Baltic project. Its open platform gathers knowledge, tools and experiences that help healthcare organisations take an eHealth solution from idea to everyday practice.

The open platform is AMBeR's own site. This page shows how the project is presented on the shared platform and leads on to it.

For the partners: AMBeR BRIDGE

AMBeR BRIDGE is the workspace for the partners' continued cooperation. It exists today as a clickable prototype with example data. The shared platform gives it what it lacks: sign-in, roles, storage and backup.

Demonstration page in the prototype. The official Interreg South Baltic logo, funding statement and disclaimer are shown on the AMBeR site itself.
