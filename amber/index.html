The implementation model

Five phases of implementing eHealth

Implementing technology is hard, and many implementations fail. No matter how good the technical solution is, it will probably not deliver the expected benefits if the people and organisations who are supposed to use it do not have a clear plan. They need to know why the solution is needed, who will use it, and how it fits into their daily work.

The implementation model and its five phases help healthcare organisations prepare for this change. It guides organisations and staff so that digital solutions can be used to their full potential.

Who is this for?

A practical model for everyone involved in implementing eHealth

The implementation model is a practical guide for taking an eHealth solution from idea to everyday practice. It describes five phases and 30 activities, each with a checklist, methods and tools, and example prompts for an AI assistant. The model builds on implementation science, the experience from the AMBeR pilots and service design.

  • 30 activities
  • Checklists, methods & AI prompts

About the project

About the AMBeR project

AMBeR – Advanced Modeling of Baltic Cancer E-Care – aims to improve cancer care in the South Baltic area by using digital solutions for home monitoring and rehabilitation, to enhance patient outcomes and accessibility.

The AMBeR project is co-financed by the Interreg South Baltic Programme 2021–2027 through the European Regional Development Fund.
