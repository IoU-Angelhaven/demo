"Someone is taking care of me"

At a glance

Organisation
Klaipėda University
Country
Lithuania
Implementing
Digital physiotherapy
Phase
3. Service Strategy & Implementation

In short

The platform only let patients give feedback with a smiley. We added text messages and updated exercise programmes often, which built trust and motivation.

The starting point

Patients followed exercise programmes on a digital platform. Contact with patients was especially important in the first two weeks.

What we noticed

The platform had very few tools for feedback. Patients could only send a smiley. That was not enough to keep in touch when patients needed it most.

What we tried

We sent text messages directly to patients’ phones. We also updated each patient’s exercise programme frequently.

What we learned

Frequent updates built trust and motivation. Patients felt that “someone is taking care of me”. [Check the wording of the quote with the site. Did it come from patients?]

Our tip for others

If the technology cannot keep the contact with patients, find another way. Personal contact matters most at the start.
