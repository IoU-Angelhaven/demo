The implementation model / Phase 3

Phase 3 of 5

Service Strategy & Implementation

This phase turns a tested solution into everyday practice. It connects solution design with full implementation by preparing the practical, financial and organisational foundations. Solution design is about exploring and testing. This phase is about practical planning and managing resources.

3

Key message

Success depends on people, technology and organisation together. Technology alone does not create change. What matters is how well organisations, teams and individuals are prepared to adopt and keep new ways of working.

The main activities are planning staff and time, planning the budget, involving stakeholders, defining measures of success, planning and running a pilot, and managing change.
