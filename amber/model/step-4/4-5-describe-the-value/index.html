The implementation model / Phase 4 / Activity 4.5

Activity 4.5

Describe the Value (Value Proposition)

A value description explains why the initiative matters, who benefits, and how the benefit is delivered and measured over time. It moves the work from a nice-to-have pilot to a strategic change that justifies continued investment.

A clear value description aligns the initiative with the organisation’s priorities, strengthens engagement among clinicians and stakeholders, supports funding and improves decisions about wider rollout.

Checklist

  • Describe why the initiative matters and who benefits: patients, staff and the organisation.
  • Identify the most valuable results of the pilot and describe them in clear terms (value proposition canvas).
  • Support the benefits with data, for example key performance indicators (KPIs).
  • Link the benefits to the strategic priorities of the organisation.
  • Describe how the benefits will be delivered and measured over time.
  • Adapt the message to each audience, especially management (stakeholder analysis).
  • Prepare clear presentations and visuals (communication plan).

Do you want to download the checklists and learn how to customise them for your own organisation? Go to Create your local guide.

Methods and tools

  • Value proposition canvas: Matches what users need with what the solution offers.
  • Key performance indicators (KPIs): Measures that show progress and results with data.
  • Benefits management: Identifies and describes benefits and follows them over time.
  • Stakeholder analysis: Shows who needs which message.
  • Communication plan: Defines messages, audiences, channels and timing.
