The implementation model / Phase 4 / Activity 4.1

Activity 4.1

Plan for the Long Term

Long-term planning looks beyond the pilot or launch. It shapes, on purpose, how the change will stay in place, grow and remain safe over time.

A good long-term plan covers clinical results and patient safety, staffing, legal and ethical duties, finances and digital inclusion. Without such a plan, a successful pilot can end when the project funding ends.

Checklist

  • Describe what the solution must achieve in the long term for clinical results and patient safety.
  • Create a roadmap: a long-term plan with milestones, responsible persons and decision points after the pilot.
  • Plan long-term staffing: who will run, support and update the solution.
  • Check legal and ethical requirements, such as data protection and patient safety rules, and plan how to follow them.
  • Plan the finances: running costs and funding after the pilot.
  • Create a plan to make sure that all patient groups, including those with low digital skills or accessibility needs, can take part.
  • Identify benefits and risks, and plan how to follow and manage them (benefits management).
  • Review the long-term plan regularly and update it when conditions change.

Do you want to download the checklists and learn how to customise them for your own organisation? Go to Create your local guide.

Methods and tools

  • Roadmap: Shows the main steps, milestones and responsibilities over several years.
  • Benefits management: Identifies the expected benefits and risks, and follows them until they are achieved.
  • Scenario analysis: Describes best-case, likely and worst-case developments to support long-term decisions.

AI prompt examples

  1. Create a roadmap

    Ask
    Create a long-term roadmap for [solution] at [organisation] for the period after the pilot. Use the attached documents. Include milestones, responsible roles, decision points, staffing needs and funding.

  2. Identify benefits and risks

    Attachyour pilot results and goals

    Ask
    Identify the main benefits and risks of continuing [solution] over the long term. Use the attached documents. For each, suggest a measure, an owner and an action to manage it.

  3. Check legal, ethical and inclusion issues

    Ask
    List the legal, ethical, accessibility and digital inclusion issues to consider when [solution] becomes part of regular care in [country or region]. Suggest how to address each one. Tell me where I need to check the current rules myself.
