The implementation model / Phase 2 / Activity 2.4

Activity 2.4

Test and Learn

Testing shows how real users handle a solution: what works and what needs to change. It lets you check assumptions early and lowers the risk of building the wrong thing.

By testing and improving in several rounds, the solution becomes more effective, easier to use and more successful in real work.

Checklist

  • Define what you want to learn and which questions you want to answer.
  • Design test situations that show typical cases and difficult cases.
  • Choose participants with different roles and experience, and get their informed consent.
  • Choose where to test: a test room, a clinic, a home or the real workplace.
  • Prepare test instructions and observation notes.
  • Test with 5–8 users in each round, and write down what you see.
  • Study the results, decide the most important improvements and make them.
  • Test the important changes again, and write down what you learned and what you recommend.

Do you want to download the checklists and learn how to customise them for your own organisation? Go to Create your local guide.

Methods and tools

  • Usability testing: Watch users do typical tasks with the solution.
  • Think-aloud testing: Users say what they think while they do the tasks.
  • Guerrilla testing: Quick, informal tests with people in real settings.
  • A/B testing: Compare two versions to see which works better.
  • Heuristic evaluation: Experts check the solution against well-known rules for good usability.
  • Pilot test: A small test in real or nearly real work.
