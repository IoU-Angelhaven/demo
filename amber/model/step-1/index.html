The implementation model / Phase 1

Phase 1 of 5

Needs Assessment & Status Quo

A needs assessment is the foundation of every implementation. It gives a clear picture of today’s situation, the problems and the needs. It also shows why change is necessary and what value the change should create. Talk to patients and other end users early, and review current workflows and challenges. This helps you avoid quick assumptions and agree on a description of the problem that is accurate and shared by those involved.

1

Key message

Do not skip this phase even if a digital solution is already available, for example a video meeting tool for patient visits. A successful implementation is more than giving staff access to a tool. Every unit has its own patient groups, workflows and staffing. Without a clear view of the current situation, a new tool can disturb daily work instead of supporting it. A tool that is not used, or is used in the wrong way, gives no efficiency gains and no better patient experience.
