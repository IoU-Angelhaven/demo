Privacy notice

Draft, not yet approved. This text is a draft for the data controller to review. The prototype holds test data only.

Who is responsible

The Innovation and Development Unit (IoU) at Ängelholms hospital, Region Skåne, is the data controller for the personal data on ehealth-implementation.eu. Contact: [contact address to be added].

What we store

For people with an account we store only: name, e-mail address, organisation, and the role you have in each project space. We also keep a log of sign-ins, role changes and edits, so that changes can be traced and undone.

If you send a form on a project's open pages, for example to fetch a course or to ask about a new language version, we store what you write there: your name, e-mail address, organisation, country and message. Only the administrators of that project can read it, and they use it to answer you.

The open pages can be read without an account. They set no tracking cookies.

Why, and on what legal basis

We use the data to let you sign in, to give you the right access in each project, and to show who wrote what in shared work. The legal basis is [to be decided by the controller, for example a task carried out in the public interest].

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms. They are not used for statistics or advertising.

Who can see your data

Administrators of a project see the name, e-mail address, organisation and role of that project's members. They never see the members of other projects. Platform administrators at IoU can see all accounts. The data is stored with our web host, Loopia AB. [Storage location and data processing agreement to be confirmed.]

How long

We keep your account until you delete it or until it is removed by an administrator. Requests for access that are declined, and messages sent from the open pages, are removed after [period to be decided]. Log entries are kept for [period to be decided]. Backups are kept for [period to be decided].

Your rights

When signed in you can see everything we store about you, download it as a file, correct your name and organisation, and delete your account, under “My account”. When you delete your account, what you have written in a project stays, shown as written by “Former member”. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
