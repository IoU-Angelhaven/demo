DEAHLKit: a course for healthcare in the digital age

Six modules, each in two editions (for healthcare managers and for healthcare professionals) about communication, vulnerable groups, accessibility, trust and leadership when health services go digital. Short films from the fictional Northviken Health and Care Centre, reflection and quizzes, in 7 languages.

Use it where your staff learn

Three ways to bring DEAHLKit to your learners

DEAHLKit is made to be reused. Every language version can be brought into the systems your organisation already uses, or used as it is, online.

Import

H5P files into your platform

One file per module and edition, imported into any learning platform that supports H5P, for example Moodle, WordPress or Canvas. You host it yourself.

Embed

SCORM in your learning system

The modules packaged for learning management systems that take SCORM, so completion can be followed in your own system.

Online

Open on DEAHL.eu

Every language version as open e-learning on the DEAHL website, with nothing to install. New language versions can be published there too, and then brought into other systems in the same ways.

The 64 films of each language are published on YouTube with subtitles and linked from the course; they are also available as 1080p files. How the course was made and where AI was used: use of AI in DEAHLKit.

A lab environment. The course for learners is on DEAHL.eu. On this site the DEAHLKit production team also runs and tests the course in a lab environment, to learn and to try out features that could be valuable: a text view for accessibility (WCAG), different ways for learners to sign in, and a short evaluation after each module. What proves useful can be offered to the consortium and to the platforms that host DEAHLKit.
Version 1.0 of each language is the version used in the pilot. Tell us if you would like to use DEAHLKit in your organisation, and we will get back to you.
Your country, your language

A new language version starts with a conversation

DEAHLKit is built to travel. Tell us about your country, your language and your learners. Then we choose together the way that fits you. There are two ways, and the same agreement for both.

Way 1

Do it yourselves

Your team makes the version with the same tools, master and guides the first seven languages were made with. We give guidance, access to the Studio and the up-to-date English master. Your team carries the work and its own licences (for example Vyond and AI voices).

Way 2

Get help from the DEAHL team

The DEAHL production team builds the version with you (translation support, audio, edited and published films and course packages) at cost price: the estimated effort in person-months (with a margin), at the Interreg Baltic Sea Region standard hourly rate for the country of the people doing the work, plus 15% for office costs such as licences and tools.

TranslationTranslation proposals for every text, with agreed terms for the whole course.
ReviewYour national team reviews every text and spoken line in the Studio.
AudioEvery character's lines are recorded in your language.
FilmsThe 64 films are rebuilt from the English master films, with the new audio and on-screen text.
PackagesThe course in your language, online or ready for your own platform.
Whichever way you choose, you agree that your language version
1 · stays in line with the English master and follows its updates;
2 · is published and made available for use in the same way as the other language versions (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with DEAHL as publisher);
3 · has a maintenance plan for future updates when the master changes;
4 · has two named national contact persons, connected to the DEAHL production team.

DEAHLKit is not a commercial product, and the course itself is free to use. Any cost covers only the development and maintenance work the DEAHL consortium carries out for a new version, during or after the project, so that the results of the Interreg project last. Draft: the terms and the maintenance plan are decided by the consortium.
